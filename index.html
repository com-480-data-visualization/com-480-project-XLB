DATA & METHODOLOGY

CineScope uses The Movies Dataset (Kaggle/TMDb) joined with MovieLens audience ratings. Monetary views include films with a positive reported revenue and a recorded production budget of at least $10,000, excluding implausible near-zero budget records. ROI is gross box-office revenue divided by production budget; it does not include marketing costs or ancillary revenues. Rating analysis requires at least 50 MovieLens ratings. All displayed financial releases occur no later than 2017.

Scope choice. Top-billed cast remains visible in film dossiers as context, but performers are not ranked as an independent driver of success. An actor's appearances coincide with budget, franchise, director and role context; treating those receipts as isolated actor influence would overstate what this observational record can establish.

Selected filmmaker portraits in the director dossier are attributed Wikimedia Commons thumbnails; each portrait links to its source and licence. Film posters are supplied by TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
